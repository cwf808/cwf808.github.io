About

Charles Fortner, a native of Ashville, Ohio, earned a B.S. in Finance from The Ohio State University and an MBA from the University of Phoenix (2006). After college he relocated to Hawaii, where for more than two decades he built ventures in wireless technology, software, and real estate. In 1998 the U.S. Small Business Administration named him National Young Entrepreneur of the Year.

Since 2005 he has worked as a wireless consultant and project manager with cellular and broadband companies on wireless site construction. He earned a Hawaii real estate license in 2007, PMP certification in 2010, and Scrum Master certification in 2020.

Community work includes helping start a hometown library, serving on a charter school board, Hawaii public-service privatization and Honolulu budgetary task forces, Past President of the Bishop Museum Association Council and the Rotary Club of Kahala Sunrise, and Assistant Governor for Rotary District 5000. In 2015 he and his partner returned primarily to Ohio to care for aging parents while keeping strong ties to Hawaii.
